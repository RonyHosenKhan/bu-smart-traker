University of Barishal Bus Routing Structure

Beside the Kirtankhola river, the bus fleet operates across three verified artery routes connecting Barishal city to the campus at Karnakati:

  • ROUTE_01 (Barishal Club): Barishal Club Limited to University of Barishal Campus (6.5 km, ~18 min). Google Maps Link ↗
  • ROUTE_02 (Natun Bazar): 7 stops via Natun Bazar, Bottola Mor, Karim Kutir (Major M A Jalil Rd direct), Chowmatha Autostand, Amtola Bus Stop, Rupatoli Roundabout, BU Campus (10.4 km, ~32 min). Google Maps Link ↗
  • ROUTE_03 (Nathullabad): Nathullabad to University of Barishal Campus (7.7 km, ~19 min). Google Maps Link ↗

Mathematical Models Used

Haversine Formula (Great-Circle Distance): d = 2R × asin(√(sin²(Δφ/2) + cos φ₁ cos φ₂ sin²(Δλ/2)))

Road Distance & ETA Model: RoadDist = Haversine × 1.28
ETA = (RoadDist / EffectiveSpeed) + (StopsRemaining × 45s dwell)

Firebase Database & Android Conversion

This web dashboard uses the exact same data schema as the native Kotlin Android project created in bu-smart-bus-tracker. To convert this directly to an Android APK, run:

npx @capacitor/cli create
npx cap add android
npx cap open android
